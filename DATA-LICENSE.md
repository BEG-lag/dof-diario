# Data license / Licencia de los datos

## English

The files in `data/` describe what the Diario Oficial de la Federación published each day: dates,
titles, issuing agencies, page numbers and links. They come from the public SIDOF service of the
Secretaría de Gobernación. Article 14, fraction VIII, of the Ley Federal del Derecho de Autor
excludes legislative, regulatory, administrative and judicial texts from copyright protection.

To the extent that this project adds anything of its own to that data (the normalized schema, the
aggregated indexes and the statistics), the author dedicates it to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Use it for anything; no
attribution is required (it is appreciated).

This is the author's understanding of the law, not legal advice. The only authoritative version of
the DOF is the official publication at [dof.gob.mx](https://dof.gob.mx).

The code is released under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
